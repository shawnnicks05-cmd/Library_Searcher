Book Title : The Craft of Research

Description : Widely recognized as one of the most successful and influential guides to research methodology ever written. The text demystifies the research process, treating it not as a solitary, mechanical chore, but as a dynamic, deeply social act of communication between researchers and an imagined community of readers.

Date : 2016

Author : Wayne C. Booth, Gregory G. Colomb, Joseph M. Williams (with updates by Joseph Bizup and William T. FitzGerald)
