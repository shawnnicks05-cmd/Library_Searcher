Book Title : Towards a New Architecture

Description : One of the most influential and controversial architectural manifestos of the 20th century. Le Corbusier passionately argues against the decorative, historical revival styles of the 19th century (like the Beaux-Arts tradition), urging architects to embrace the clean, functional aesthetic of the industrial age. Famously declaring that "a house is a machine for living in," he points to mass-produced ocean liners, airplanes, and automobiles as the true models for modern design efficiency. The book lays the theoretical groundwork for the International Style and modern urban planning.

Date : 1923
