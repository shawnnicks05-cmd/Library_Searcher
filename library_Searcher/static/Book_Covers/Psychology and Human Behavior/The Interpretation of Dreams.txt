Book Title : The Interpretation of Dreams

Description : The Interpretation of Dreams (German: Die Traumdeutung) is a seminal 1899 book by Austrian neurologist and psychoanalyst Sigmund Freud. The work introduced Freud’s groundbreaking theory that dreams are a form of wish fulfillment revealing unconscious desires, laying the foundation for psychoanalytic thought.
