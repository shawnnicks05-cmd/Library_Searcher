
Book Title: Thinking, Fast and Slow

Description: Thinking, Fast and Slow is a 2011 book by psychologist and Nobel laureate Daniel Kahneman. It summarizes decades of research in cognitive psychology and behavioral economics, explaining how two systems of thought—fast, intuitive thinking and slow, deliberate reasoning—shape human judgment and decision-making.
