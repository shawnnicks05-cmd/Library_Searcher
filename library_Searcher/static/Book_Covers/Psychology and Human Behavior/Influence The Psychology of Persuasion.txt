Book Title : Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion

Description : Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion is a 1984 nonfiction book by social psychologist Robert Cialdini. It explores the psychological mechanisms that underlie compliance and persuasion, providing a framework for understanding how people are influenced in everyday interactions. The book is widely regarded as a foundational text in marketing, sales, and behavioral psychology.
