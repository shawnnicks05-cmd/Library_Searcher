Book Title : Campbell Biology

Description : The sixth edition of this world-renowned textbook solidified its status as the gold standard for introductory biology. This edition heavily emphasized the integration of evolutionary theory across all biological scales and introduced groundbreaking discoveries of the early 2000s, particularly the initial mapping of the human genome. It combines exceptional narrative clarity with structured thematic pathways to guide students from microscopic molecular bonds to complex ecological systems.

Date : 2002

Author : Neil A. Campbell, Jane B. Reece
