Book Title : On the Origin of Species

Description : One of the most important scientific works ever written, this book revolutionized humanity's understanding of the natural world. Darwin introduces the scientific theory that populations of living organisms evolve over generations through a process known as Natural Selection. Written for a broad audience, the text compiles a massive array of empirical evidence gathered from breeding experiments, geographical distributions, and the fossil record to demonstrate that all diversity of life shares a common lineage.
