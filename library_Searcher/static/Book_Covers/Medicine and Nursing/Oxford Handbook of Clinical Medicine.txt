Book Title :Oxford Handbook of Clinical Medicine (Eleventh Edition)

Description : Fondly known as the "Cheese and Onion" handbook due to its traditional green and yellow cover, this pocket-sized reference guide is the gold standard for clinical rotations and junior doctors. It balances core medical facts with patient-centered advice, offering step-by-step diagnostic pathways, immediate management guidelines for emergencies, and witty, philosophical reflections on the art of practicing medicine.
