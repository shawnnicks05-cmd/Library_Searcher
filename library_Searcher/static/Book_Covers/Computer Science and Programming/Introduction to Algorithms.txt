Book Title : Introduction to Algorithms

Description : Introduction to Algorithms, often abbreviated as CLRS after its authors — Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, and Clifford Stein — is a comprehensive textbook on algorithm design and analysis. Published by The MIT Press, it is regarded as one of the most influential and widely used texts in computer science education worldwide.
