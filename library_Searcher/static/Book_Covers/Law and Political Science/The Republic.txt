Book Title : The Republic

Description : One of the foundational intellectual texts of Western civilization. Structured as a Socratic dialogue, the book features Socrates debating various Athenian citizens and philosophers to define the nature of absolute justice. To understand justice within an individual, Socrates builds a hypothetical "ideal city-state" (Kallipolis), exploring education architectures, censorship, the abolition of private property among rulers, and governance by an elite intellectual class.

Date : 375 BC

Author : Plato
