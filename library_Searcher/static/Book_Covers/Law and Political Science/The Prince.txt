Book Title : The Prince

Description : One of the most famous and controversial political treatises in Western history. Written as a practical manual for rulers rather than an idealistic vision of an ethical society, it rejects traditional Christian morality in statecraft. Machiavelli introduces a deeply pragmatic view of power politics, arguing that a ruler's primary duty is to maintain the stability of the state and secure power by any means necessary—giving rise to the term "Machiavellian."

Date : 1532

Author : Niccolò Machiavelli
