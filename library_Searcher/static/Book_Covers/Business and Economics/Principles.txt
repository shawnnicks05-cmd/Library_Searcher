Book Title : Principles

Description : An interactive companion workbook designed to help readers operationalize the philosophies found in the author's primary text, Principles. Instead of merely reading about success strategies, this journal provides open-ended prompts, structured thought exercises, and frameworks to guide individuals in reflecting on their own experiences, diagnosing mistakes, and creating a personalized set of rules for decision-making in life, career, and relationships.
