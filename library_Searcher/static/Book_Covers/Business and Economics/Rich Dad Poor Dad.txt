Book Title : Rich Dad Poor Dad:

Description : A foundational personal finance book that challenges common myths about wealth. Written in an anecdotal style, it contrasts the mindsets of two father figures in the author's life: his biological father (a highly educated but financially struggling government worker) and his best friend's father (a self-made millionaire and entrepreneur). The book advocates for financial literacy, financial independence, and building wealth by investing in assets rather than liabilities.

Date : April 1, 1997 (Original publication)

Author : Robert T. Kiyosaki (with Sharon L. Lechter, C.P.A.)
