Book Title : The Intelligent Investor

Description : Widely considered the "bible" of investing, this seminal text outlines the principles of "value investing"—an investment approach centered on analyzing a company’s fundamental value and buying its stock only when there is a significant safety margin. Rather than chasing market trends, speculation, or short-term trading patterns, the book teaches emotional discipline, long-term thinking, and a realistic understanding of market psychology.

Date : 1949

Author : Benjamin Graham
